# Privacy Policy

FFishAsia respects your privacy.

## What the app accesses
- Camera: used only to place and preview AR models in your surroundings.
- Local storage: used to save downloaded model files on your device.
- Anonymous product analytics: app interactions such as catalog views, model details, downloads, 3D previews, and AR starts may be sent with random installation and session identifiers, app/build version, OS version, locale, and fixed model attributes.

## What the app does not do
- No account registration or login
- No collection of personal profile data
- No upload of your photos, camera feed, or downloaded model files to our servers
- No third-party advertising SDKs
- No advertising, cross-app tracking, search terms, or free-form text in analytics

## Model downloads
When you choose to download a model, the file is downloaded to your device so you can open it later, including offline for already downloaded content.

## Data retention
Downloaded model files remain on your device until you delete them inside the app or remove the app.

Analytics installation and session identifiers are HMAC-hashed before database storage. Analytics data is used only to understand feature adoption and reliability. Cloudflare provides the endpoint and database infrastructure and processes standard network request information to deliver and protect the service.
