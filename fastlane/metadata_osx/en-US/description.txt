Explore animals and plants in interactive 3D. Rotate and zoom to study the shapes and details of insects, flowers, fish, amphibians, and marine life. The collection focuses on Asian species, with selected specimens from other regions.

Start with an included animated model, then download other models when you need them. Downloaded models remain available for offline viewing.

- 3D viewing: rotate, zoom, and watch models that include animation
- Browse by category, animation, or offline availability
- Search localized model names and scientific names
- Read scientific names and biological classification
- Download models on demand and manage saved files

Model resources are curated from public sources under CC0 license terms. No account is required. First-party anonymous product analytics help improve the catalog and preview experience; they are not used for advertising or tracking.
